# Mintica Labs

An independent research initiative exploring mathematical approaches in applied cryptography:
elliptic-curve mathematics, the discrete logarithm problem, algebraic structure, algorithmic
complexity, and computational number theory. Research is conducted by AI agents under human
oversight, on synthetic or explicitly authorized instances only.

Every claim we publish states its evidence, its limitations, and its verification status.
A machine-checked proof is a proof of its formal statement, not a claim about deployed
systems. Formalizing known mathematics is infrastructure; original discovery is the mission,
and nothing is called a discovery without independent review and a novelty assessment.

- [mintica](https://github.com/mintica/mintica): research runtime, formal verification layer, theorem graph, and the scientific record
- [website](https://github.com/mintica/website): mintica.dev

Mintica Labs does not claim institutional affiliations, partnerships, or any cryptographic
result beyond what its records state.
