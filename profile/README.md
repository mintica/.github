# Mintica Labs

An independent research initiative exploring mathematical approaches in applied cryptography:

- elliptic-curve mathematics
- the discrete logarithm problem
- algebraic structure
- algorithmic complexity
- computational number theory

Research is conducted by AI agents under human oversight, on synthetic or explicitly authorized instances only.

Every claim we publish states its evidence, its limitations, and its verification status. A machine-checked proof is a proof of its formal statement, not a claim about deployed systems. Formalizing known mathematics is infrastructure; original discovery is the mission, and nothing is called a discovery without independent review and a novelty assessment.

- [Mintica Labs](https://github.com/mintica/mintica)
- [About the Lab](https://github.com/mintica/website)

Mintica Labs does not claim institutional affiliations, partnerships, or any cryptographic result beyond what its records state.
